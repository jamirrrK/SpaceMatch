# SpaceMatch Tutorial Narration Script

**Status:** Narration draft; no AI voice has been generated.  
**Estimated runtime:** 3:00-3:15 at a calm 125-135 words per minute, with short visual holds.  
**Voice direction:** Friendly, professional, clear English for Filipino and international audiences. Pronounce "SpaceMatch" as two words. Keep the pace conversational and avoid salesy emphasis.

## Scene 1 - Welcome (0:00-0:18)

**Visual:** Current homepage and SpaceMatch mark. Let the real featured spaces or empty state appear naturally.

**Narration:**

"Welcome to SpaceMatch. Explore rental spaces, compare the details that matter, and manage your SpaceMatch account in one place. This short tour will show you the tools that are available today."

## Scene 2 - Create an account (0:18-0:42)

**Visual:** Registration page; show owner and renter as the public account options, then the login form.

**Narration:**

"To get started, create an account as a renter or a property owner. Enter your details, choose a secure password, and sign in. SpaceMatch then directs you to the dashboard for your account. Administrator accounts are managed separately."

## Scene 3 - Explore spaces (0:42-1:14)

**Visual:** Search the real public listings by location; briefly apply price, type, amenity, and capacity filters, then sort results.

**Narration:**

"Browse published spaces that are currently marked available. Search by property name or location, then refine the results by monthly price, property type, capacity, and amenities. You can sort the matches by newest, lowest price, or highest price. Your filters stay in the page address, so you can revisit or share the same search."

## Scene 4 - Compare a listing (1:14-1:42)

**Visual:** Open a real public property detail page; show its images, location, spaces, monthly rates, capacity, and amenities.

**Narration:**

"Open a listing to review its photos and location. Compare the published spaces, monthly prices, capacity, and listed amenities. Availability is shown from the listing data, and unpublished or unavailable spaces are not presented as bookable."

Favorites and property inquiries are not implemented. Do not record or use the supplied draft narration for these features.

## Scene 6 - Viewings and reservations (Excluded)

Viewing appointments and reservation requests are not implemented. Do not show a date picker, approval, booking confirmation, or reservation status.

## Scene 7 - Owner workspace (1:42-2:26)

**Visual:** Use the isolated owner account to create a property, add a rental space and amenity, upload images, then publish the space and property.

**Narration:**

"Property owners can manage their own listings from the owner workspace. Create a property with its location and details, then add one or more rental spaces with prices, capacity, and amenities. Upload clear photos, choose the cover image, and review the listing before publishing. SpaceMatch checks that a property has a published space before it can appear in public discovery."
